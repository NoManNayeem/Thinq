# Payroll Calendar 2027

Status: Approved, October 15, 2026
CONFIDENTIAL: Finance only

- Payroll runs on the 25th of each month, or the previous working day.
- Cut-off for changes: the 15th of each month.
- Bonus payments: March and September payrolls.
- Year-end adjustments close on December 10.
