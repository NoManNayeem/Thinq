# Illustrations

`coffee`, `reading-side` and `sitting-reading` are from **Open Doodles** by Pablo Stanley
(https://www.opendoodles.com), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication):
free for commercial and personal use, no attribution required.

They were optimised with SVGO and recoloured to Thinq's palette (`-light` for light
surfaces, `-dark` for dark ones).
